Particle in a One-Dimensional Box

Interactive simulation for a quantum physics project.

Energy Eₙ
Energy in eV
Internal nodes
Formula
Eₙ = n²h²/(8mL²)

Wavefunction ψₙ(x)

Probability Density |ψₙ(x)|²

How to use: Move n from 1 to 8. Notice that the number of internal nodes becomes n−1 and the energy increases as n². Increase L and notice that the energy decreases as 1/L².
